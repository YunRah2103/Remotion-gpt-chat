## 1. Locked format and narrative
450 frames, frame index 0–449, 30fps, **15.000 s**, portrait 1080×1920. Deterministic poses only, evaluated as pure functions of integer frame (including independent chunk renders); no accumulated frame-state or nondeterministic physics in the final renderer.

| Frames (inclusive) | Intent | Mandatory event |
|---|---|---|
| 0–89 | HERO / complete product | Slow restrained yaw and forward dolly; three fans clearly readable. Product header appears without occlusion. |
| 90–179 | COOLER RELEASE | Fans and shroud move away from cooler in explicit stages, preserving recognisable assembly. |
| 180–329 | INTERNAL EXPLODED | Heat sink/heatpipes, die, memory, PCB and backplate separate sequentially with controlled ease; progressive visibility, never arbitrary disappearance. |
| 330–449 | DECONSTRUCTED | Complete layered hardware remains in frame with slow parallax orbit; final `DECONSTRUCTED` title. All parts persist. |

Use ease-in-out quintic or cubic smoothstep with endpoints clamped. From/to movement with absolute frame interpolation. Strictly no hard-cut between the four intervals.

## 2. World axes, scale, origin, camera
- **Units: metres.** +X right along the card's long edge toward the far fan, +Y UP from PCIe fingers toward upper shroud edge, +Z **out of the triple-fan face toward the viewer**. Z=0 at central PCB midplane. Right-handed Y-up GLB/glTF coordinates; **NO baked Blender Z-up** / negative-scale axis remapping; exporter must convert to Y-up.
- Envelope at rest: X `[-1.450,+1.450]`, Y `[-0.620,+0.620]`, Z `[-0.245,+0.245]` (or closest tolerances ±3%); intentional metal I/O bracket can extend toward one edge but must be identified in metadata. **X=0,Y=0,Z=0** is overall card envelope centre. PCIe fingers point DOWN (-Y). Three fans are side-by-side on +Z face and turn about their individual +Z shafts. Fan centres approximately X=[-0.97,0,+0.97] metres and Y≈+0.04.
- For external dimensions and centre alignment, use **1 scene metre = 100 physical mm** (thus model X=2.90, Y=1.24, Z=0.49). The geometry is display-scale 10× actual metres, intentionally; asset metadata must say `sceneUnitsPerMillimetre:0.01`. Never confuse with physical SI units.
- Camera design orthographic for guaranteed mobile safe frame: at opening `[0.28,0.14,10.5]`, aim at `[0,0,0]`, ortho view height **6.55 scene units**, therefore portrait width≈3.684 units. Use gentle ±0.5 unit lateral travel and yaw ≤12° through hero. At exploded stage position transitions toward `[2.0,0.55,11.8]`, looking at `[0,0,0]`, with ortho view height widened to **7.3 units** (width 4.106). Center/scale can be adjusted only after actual frame QA demonstrates no clipping.
- Safe region: geometry projected pixel X=75..1005, Y=390..1460 at all frames; header top Y≈155..315; final label bottom Y≈1550..1730. **NO critical card detail cropped**. Do not assume front-perfect camera throughout; subtle 3/4 front view is preferred when parts separate.
