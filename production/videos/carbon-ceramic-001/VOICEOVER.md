# Carbon-ceramic brakes — narration to record or generate separately

## Primary script (approval required)

Every hard stop turns speed into heat.
Brake pads squeeze the spinning disc, and temperatures climb fast.
Carbon-ceramic discs are built to withstand repeated thermal stress while keeping braking response consistent.
They're also much lighter than traditional cast iron.
The advantage is dependable performance, stop after stop.

## Shorter option

Braking converts motion into heat.
Pads squeeze the disc, and its temperature rises.
Carbon-ceramic brakes are built to tolerate repeated heavy stops and resist fade.
They're lighter than cast iron, too.
That's the advantage: consistent performance under demanding use.

## Engineering notes

- The text describes road-car carbon-ceramic composites, NOT racing carbon-carbon discs.
- Carbon-ceramic resists high-temperature fading; do not claim it is immune to all fade, wear or overheating.
- Do not promise universally shorter stopping distance: tyre traction and other vehicle systems matter.
- Heat palettes in the video are illustrative, not measured temperatures.
